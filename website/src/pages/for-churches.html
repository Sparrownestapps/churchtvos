For churches

Made for the way a church week actually runs

Kids rooms on Sunday, lobby screens all week, slides in the sanctuary, maybe a second campus across town. Church TV OS is one place to run all of it.

Kids ministry

The right lesson in the right room

Build each week's playlist once: welcome video, countdown, song, lesson video, wrap-up. Schedule the series, and every Sunday the room's TV has the next week ready.

  • Leaders press Play. The screen shows what is coming up, so nobody is surprised.
  • A countdown gets a room settled and ready.
  • Different rooms can run different playlists on the same morning.
  • Content is saved on the TV ahead of time, so a Wi-Fi hiccup doesn't empty a room of preschoolers.

Lobby and hallway signage

Announcements that never go stale

Put up this week's events, a welcome message, or a loop of photos and videos. Set the hours it plays and forget it.

  • Text signs made right in the dashboard, in your colours
  • Pictures and videos on a weekly schedule
  • Group your lobby screens so one change updates them all
  • Portrait screens for a welcome-desk or hallway display
  • Dark when nothing is scheduled, never out of date

Sanctuary slides

Lyrics, sermon points and announcements

Build the Sunday deck during the week: welcome, announcements, songs, scripture, sermon points, offering, a countdown to start. On Sunday a volunteer steps through it with the remote or their phone.

  • Song lyrics from your library, split for you, in your order
  • Your theme and a background that suits the day
  • Import the PDF or PowerPoint you already have
  • A volunteer can run it from the operator role without being able to change it
  • Jump to any slide when the service goes off script

Multi-campus

One system, every campus

Each campus is a site with its own rooms and its own time zone. Share what is shared, and keep local what is local.

  • Owners and admins see everything
  • Editors and operators can be limited to certain modules and certain campuses
  • Your logo and colours on every screen
  • See every screen, and whether it is online, in one list
