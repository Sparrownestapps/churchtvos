How it works

From box to Sunday in three steps

  1. Pair a TV

    Open the app on a TV. It shows a six-character code. Type it into the dashboard, pick the room, and the TV is yours.

    NCB Z3C

  2. Build your content

    Upload videos and pictures, write songs and signs, or import a PDF or PowerPoint. Do it on a computer, or from your phone or Mac.

  3. Schedule it, then run it

    Put it on the calendar. Then run the room from the TV remote or from your phone: next, back, jump, blank, even a message to the screen.

Remote control

Run the room from where you're standing

No more walking to the front to press a button. Open the app on your phone or Mac and you are holding the remote for any screen.

  • Next and back, or jump straight to any slide in the list

  • Play a deck or playlist right now, for an hour or until you stop it

Questions, answered plainly

What TV hardware do I need?

A Fire TV device, or a Google TV / Android TV device, plugged into the TV. Church TV OS runs as an app on it. Newer Fire TV sticks that run Amazon's Vega OS cannot run it.

What happens if the internet goes down on Sunday?

The TV downloads what is scheduled ahead of time, so it keeps playing from saved content. It catches up when the connection returns.

Can volunteers run things without being able to break them?

Yes. The operator role can use the remote (next, back, blank, play now, messages) but cannot edit playlists, decks, schedules or screens. Editors can change content for just the modules and campuses you choose.

Can I bring in my existing PowerPoint or PDF?

Yes. PDF pages become full-screen picture slides that keep your layout exactly. PowerPoint slides become editable text slides with your notes, and slides that are only a picture stay pictures. Animations and video effects are not carried over, so for the exact look, export from PowerPoint to PDF first.

Does it handle song lyrics?

Yes. You keep a song library for your church, the words are split into slides for you, and you set the order (verse, chorus, verse, chorus, bridge). Kids and Slides share the same library. You add the lyrics you are licensed to use.

Can one TV do different jobs on different days?

Yes. For example, Signage Monday to Saturday and Kids on Sunday morning, set on a weekly schedule for that screen.

Is our content kept separate from other churches?

Yes. Everything belongs to your church, and nothing is shared between churches. Read the privacy policy for details.

How much does it cost?

Every new church can start with a free trial. Simple pricing, per module and per TV, is coming. We have not set the numbers yet, and we would like to hear what your church needs first. See the pricing page.

Can I try it without a TV?

You can create a free account and explore the dashboard right away. The TV app also has a built-in demo mode that needs no account, so you can see what a screen looks like.
