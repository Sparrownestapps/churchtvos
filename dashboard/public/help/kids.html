Kids module

Last updated 2026-10-20

The Kids module plays your curriculum in each kids room. Leaders are in control: the TV shows what is coming up and waits for someone to press Play.

Playlists

Open Kids, Playlists and press New playlist. Give it a name such as "Elementary - Week 3", choose who it is available to (all sites or one), then add items in order. The arrows move an item up or down, and the cross removes it.

What you can add

Video
Pick from your Kids library. It plays to the end, then the next item is ready.
Slide (image)
Pick an image. By default it stays up until the leader moves on. Type a number in "Auto-advance after (sec)" to make it move on by itself.
Countdown
A big timer. Set the heading (for example "Service starts in") and the minutes and seconds. When it hits zero it moves on.
Lyrics
Type or paste a song title and the words. Leave a blank line between slides, and each block becomes one slide on the screen.
YouTube
Choose a video, or paste any YouTube link (and optionally a start time like 12:30). If you add your YouTube channel ID in Settings, you can pick from your recent live streams, and use Live now, which plays whatever your channel is streaming when the item comes up.
Live stream
A live video link ending in .m3u8 (HLS). Choose how long to show it, or leave it blank to stay until the leader moves on. Use Test link to check it. Pick "Skip it" or "Show waiting for feed" for when the stream is down. Sound is off unless you turn it on. Needs app 1.1.0 or newer.

YouTube items do not rotate on portrait screens. See Portrait screens.

Save with Save playlist. Duplicate on the list makes a copy you can tweak for next week.

What leaders see and do on the TV

On a Kids TV the home screen shows the room name and the playlist that is scheduled, with its items listed and a Play button.

  • OK or Play starts the playlist. During a video, OK or Play/Pause pauses and resumes it.
  • Right (or Next) moves to the next item. Left (or Previous) goes back.
  • Back stops and returns to the home screen.

Scheduling a Kids playlist

Kids playlists are scheduled per location: the TV in that location plays what is scheduled for it. You can schedule from the Calendar (best for repeats) or from Kids, Schedule.

  1. On Kids, Schedule, choose a playlist and a start date and time. Add an end time if you want one.
  2. Tick the locations (only locations tagged Kids appear), then press Add to schedule.

A playlist stays on a location's TV until its end time, or until the next scheduled playlist starts. TVs download everything scheduled in the next 14 days ahead of time, so the room works even if the internet is down on Sunday morning. The list below shows what is Showing now, Upcoming or Replaced.

Repeat every week, with a rotation

If your curriculum runs week by week, make a repeating schedule instead of scheduling each Sunday by hand.

  1. Open the Calendar, drag across a Sunday time and choose Kids playlist.
  2. Under Repeat, choose Every week. The first playlist is "Week 1".
  3. Add more playlists for Week 2, Week 3 and so on, in order. Choose whether the rotation stops at the end or repeats from the start.
  4. Save with Create repeating schedule.

Each time it comes round, the TV gets the next playlist in the list. On the calendar the blocks are labelled like "Week 3: Noah". Manage them under Repeating schedules.

Skip or change one date

Click one occurrence on the calendar. You can Change this date to a different playlist, or Skip this date (a holiday, a special service). Skipping makes the rotation wait, so the skipped week's playlist moves to the next date. A changed date still uses up its week. Back to the rotation undoes it.

A one-time schedule on the same time replaces a repeating one, so you can always override a single Sunday without touching the series.
