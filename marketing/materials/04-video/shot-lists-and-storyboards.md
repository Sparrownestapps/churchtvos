# Shot lists and storyboards

Text storyboards (frame, camera, action, audio). Use as a checklist on shoot day.

## Gear and set-up
* Phone camera on a tripod, 4K 30fps, landscape for the hero (also shoot a 9:16 safe version by framing the action in the centre third).
* Screen recording: QuickTime (Mac) or the phone's built-in recorder for the dashboard; use a fresh test church. Zoom the browser to 125 percent so text is readable on a phone.
* TV recording: do not film a TV with a phone close up (moiré). Capture the TV app via HDMI capture or use the demo-mode screenshots/video; film the TV from 2 metres with a slower shutter only for "TV in room" shots.
* Light: daylight hallway, no overheads on the TV glass. Audio: lavalier or a quiet room.
* Release forms: none needed (no people but Keith). If a volunteer appears, written permission and no children.

### Storyboard (hero)
1. **Frame 1:** Hallway. Keith stands left of frame, TV on the wall right, chair with laptop and cables front-left. Audio: soft room tone. VO line one.
2. **Frame 2:** Cable macro. Rack focus from cables to the HDMI port as a hand brings the stick in.
3. **Frame 3:** TV shows the pairing screen with a six-character code, big and centred. Hold.
4. **Frame 4:** Split: left, a laptop dashboard typing the code; right, the TV flipping to the Nursery countdown. (If split is hard, cut A then B.)
5. **Frame 5:** Calendar drag: a coloured block lands on Sunday 9:00. A small tick appears.
6. **Frame 6:** Countdown 0:09 on the TV. Hand reaches in and unplugs the router in the background (a quick visual of "internet drops"), TV keeps counting. *(Film only if you have actually tested this.)*
7. **Frame 7:** Phone in a hand. Thumb hits Next; TV changes; hits Blank; TV goes black; hits Next; returns.
8. **Frame 8:** Lobby: the "This Sunday" sign; a quick dissolve to a Slides lyric; a dissolve to a Kids Check-in sign.
9. **Frame 9:** Keith, same framing as Frame 1; chair empty; TV shows the welcome sign.
10. **Frame 10:** End card.

## 30-second and 15-second: shot reuse
