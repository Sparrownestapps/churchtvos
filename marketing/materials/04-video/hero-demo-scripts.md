# Hero demo scripts: 75 seconds, 30 seconds, 15 seconds

One story, three lengths. Record the 75s first; the 30s and 15s are cut from the same footage.
**Voice:** Keith, to camera for the open and close, voice-over for the middle. Warm, unhurried, a little dry.
**Music:** none, or royalty-free instrumental with a licence file. **No copyrighted worship songs** (Content ID will claim or mute it).
**Captions:** burn in on all cuts.
**Safety:** no identifiable children. Show screens, hands, the back of a TV, a phone. Use demo-mode content or your own original content.
**Claims check:** every line below is true today. Do not add price, platform dates, iPhone/Mac apps, Planning Center or NDI.
