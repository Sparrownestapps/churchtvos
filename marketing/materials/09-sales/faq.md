## The basics

**What is Church TV OS?**
A system that runs the screens at your church on the TVs and streaming devices you already have: kids rooms, lobby and hallway signs, and Sunday slides. You build and schedule in a web dashboard, pair each TV with a six-character code, and run the room from your phone.

**What TVs does it run on?**
Fire TV, Google TV (and Android TV), and Roku. LG and Samsung are coming soon. You need a TV with an HDMI port and one of those devices plugged in, or a TV that already has one built in.

**Who is it for?**
Kids pastors and directors, tech and media teams, church administrators and executive pastors, and small churches with one TV who want announcements all week and lyrics on Sunday.

**Who made it?**
Keith Sparrow, a worship pastor in Cambridge, Ontario, who leads the music and tech teams at Forward Church. It began as a way to run Forward Church's kids rooms. It's a separate product, and Forward Church is one of its customers.

## Setup

**How do I set up a TV?**
Install Church TV OS on the TV. It shows a six-character code. Type that code into Screens, Pair a TV in the dashboard at app.churchtvos.com, choose the room, and save. The TV then shows what you schedule.

**How long does it take?**
Pairing takes about a minute. Building your first playlist or sign takes about ten minutes.

**Do I need to install anything on my phone?**
No. The dashboard works in your phone's browser, including the remote. iPhone and Mac apps are coming soon.

**Can I try it without a TV?**
Yes. You can create a free account and explore the dashboard. The TV app also has a built-in demo mode that needs no account (Menu on Fire TV; the Settings button on the pairing screen on Google TV).

## Sundays

**What happens if the internet goes down?**
TVs download what's scheduled ahead of time, so they keep playing from saved content and catch up when the connection returns. Sending a message from your phone needs the internet to arrive.

**Can volunteers run it without breaking it?**
Yes. The operator role can use the remote (next, back, jump, blank, play now, messages) but can't edit playlists, decks, schedules or screens.

**Can one TV do different jobs on different days?**
Yes. For example, Signage Monday to Saturday and Kids on Sunday morning, on a weekly schedule for that screen.

**Does it do countdowns and song lyrics?**
Yes. Countdowns have a heading ("Service starts in"), a time, and what happens at zero. Songs live in a song library, split into slides with a play order you set. You add the lyrics you're licensed to use.

**Can I use a PowerPoint or PDF?**
Yes. PDF pages become full-screen picture slides that keep your layout. PowerPoint slides become editable text slides with your notes, and slides that are only a picture stay pictures. Animations and video effects aren't carried over, so for the exact look, export to PDF first.

**What about portrait TVs?**
Set the orientation per screen and the content turns to fit. YouTube videos don't rotate on portrait screens. On Roku, video and live streams are skipped on portrait screens.

## Content and safety

**Whose music, videos and lyrics does it play?**
Yours. Church TV OS plays what you upload. Your church is responsible for the licences for lyrics, music, video and curriculum, for example a CCLI licence. Church TV OS doesn't supply copyrighted content, and it doesn't have a built-in curriculum library or a link to any curriculum company.

**Is our content kept separate from other churches?**
Yes. Everything belongs to your church and nothing is shared between churches. See the [privacy policy](https://app.churchtvos.com/privacy.html).

**What data does the TV app collect?**
A random device identifier, the device model, the app version and the last-connected time, tied to your church's screen. No names, contacts, location or advertising IDs. No ads and no analytics SDKs in the TV app.

**Is it safe for kids rooms?**
Children see what's on a screen but never use the app, and no child data is collected. Your church still sets its own policies about what's on the screens and who can change it.

## Honest limits

* **Newer Fire TV sticks running Vega OS can't run it.** Other Fire TV devices can.
* **The Roku channel is new.** Some things differ: YouTube items aren't supported on Roku.
* **LG and Samsung aren't live yet.** There's a waitlist; no date promised.
* **No integration with Planning Center, CCLI, Orange or other curriculum companies today.**
* **It isn't a replacement for a big-sanctuary production rig.** It's for rooms, chapels, overflow, satellites and secondary screens, and for churches that don't have a production team.
* **Auto-launch** when the TV turns on is a Fire TV option only. On Google TV you open the app yourself.
* **iPhone and Mac apps** are coming soon. The phone remote today is the dashboard in your browser.
