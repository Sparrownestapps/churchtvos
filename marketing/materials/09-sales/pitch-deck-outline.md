# Pitch deck outline (10 slides) and speaker notes

For a 15-minute conversation with a church's kids pastor, tech director or leadership team (also usable on a screen share). A matching PowerPoint is in `church-tv-os-pitch-deck.pptx` (built from this outline; edit freely).
Honest, short, no logos you don't own, no price (use `[PRICE]`), no customer count.

| # | Title | On the slide | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Church TV OS | Logo, "Every screen at your church, run from your phone." Live on Fire TV, Google TV, Roku. | "I'm Keith. I'm a worship pastor in Cambridge, Ontario. I built this to run our kids rooms." |
| 2 | Sunday, ten to nine | A cable tangle and a laptop. "The countdown video is on a laptop in the car." | A concrete Sunday scene. Ask: "Does that sound familiar?" |

## Appendix slides (keep hidden unless asked)
* A1: Roles and campuses: owner, admin, editor, operator by module and campus.
* A2: Compatibility matrix: Fire TV (Fire OS, not Vega OS), Google TV, Android TV, Roku (new).
* A3: Pricing structure: per module, per TV: [PRICE]. Trial. Founding offer.
* A4: Security and data: separate per church, no ads, device ID only (`privacy.html`).
* A5: Content licences: the church holds them; Church TV OS doesn't supply copyrighted content.
* A6: Comparison by category (`comparison-sheet.md` Part A).

## Delivery notes
* Show the demo, don't narrate it: open the TV app in demo mode (Menu on Fire TV; Settings button on Google TV).
* Leave 5 minutes for their questions. Use `objection-handling.md`.
* Slides are images of the real demo screens. No children, no copyrighted content.
