# Objection handling sheet (internal)

For calls, demos, DMs and email replies. Principle: agree with the worry, give the honest answer, offer to prove it on their TV. Never pressure. If it's not a fit, say so and part friends.
No competitor names in anything you send. If a prospect names a tool, answer about the category and your own product.

## The big ones

**"We already use free tools (a laptop, a USB stick, YouTube, a free kids player)."**
*Hear:* it works, it's free, and I don't want a new thing.
*Say:* "If it's working, keep it. The question I'd ask is what it costs you on Sunday: who carries the laptop, who's the technician in the room, what happens when it fails. A free tool is a good answer for one room. It gets harder at three rooms and a lobby."
*Offer:* "Try one room for a month. If it isn't calmer, you've lost an evening."
*Don't say:* anything negative about a named free tool.

**"It's too expensive."**
*Say:* "Fair. Pricing is per module and per TV, so you only pay for what you use: [PRICE]. A Fire TV, Google TV or Roku device usually costs far less than other boxes. If you tell me how many rooms you've got, I'll work out the real number with you."
*Offer:* "[FOUNDING OFFER] if you're one of the first churches." *(Only if real.)*
*Don't say:* any price until pricing is decided, and never claim it's "the cheapest".

**"What if the internet drops on Sunday?"**
*Say:* "TVs download what's scheduled ahead of time and keep playing from saved content, then catch up. The one thing that needs the internet is a message you send from your phone to the screen. Want to test it? Unplug the router while it's playing."

**"Will our volunteers be able to use it?"**
*Say:* "That's the design. Volunteers get the operator role: Next, Back, Blank, Play now and messages. They can't edit anything. Want to hand a volunteer a phone and see?"

**"We need it to work with [curriculum] / Planning Center / CCLI."**
*Say:* "You upload what you're licensed to use, so any video or slides you have will play. There's no built-in curriculum library and no Planning Center or CCLI integration today, and I won't pretend there is. If an integration would decide it for you, tell me, it helps me decide what to build."

**"What about Apple TV?"**
*Say:* "It isn't on Apple TV today. It runs on Fire TV, Google TV and Roku, and LG and Samsung are coming. If you've already invested in Apple TVs, I'd tell you to keep them."

**"Is it proven? Who else uses it?"**
*Say (true today):* "It started as the system for Forward Church's kids rooms, where I'm the worship pastor, and it's now open to any church. I'm new, so I'd rather you test it than trust a logo. [TESTIMONIAL / reference calls, only if real.]"
*Don't say:* "trusted by churches everywhere", any customer count you can't prove, or any rating.

**"We're small. One TV."**
*Say:* "That's a good fit. Announcements all week, lyrics on Sunday, same TV. Start with one TV and one module." [If a small-church or church-plant offer exists, describe it here.]

**"We've got ProPresenter / a production team."**
*Say:* "Keep it for the main room. Church TV OS isn't trying to replace that. It's for the kids rooms, the lobby and the rooms that don't have an operator."

## The practical ones

**"Newer Fire stick?"** "If it runs Amazon's Vega OS, it can't run it. Standard Fire OS devices can. Google TV and Roku are another option."
**"Does it work on Roku?"** "Yes, and the Roku channel is new. Some things differ (YouTube isn't supported on Roku). Try it and tell me what you hit."
**"LG / Samsung?"** "Coming soon, no date. I'll email you once if you join the list. A Fire, Google TV or Roku device in the HDMI port works on those TVs today."
**"Portrait screens?"** "Yes, per screen. YouTube doesn't rotate on portrait."
**"Can I import PowerPoint?"** "Yes. PDF stays pixel-perfect. PowerPoint becomes editable slides; animations don't carry over."
**"What if you disappear?"** "Fair question about a solo product. [ANSWER: data export, what happens to content, continuity plan. Needs Keith.]"
**"Who owns our content?"** "You do. Nothing is shared between churches. Privacy policy is at app.churchtvos.com/privacy.html."
**"Can the phone remote work on an iPhone?"** "In Safari today. A native iPhone app is coming soon."
**"Do you integrate with Orange / [curriculum]?"** "No integration. You bring the content you're licensed to use."
**"Support?"** "It's me. [SUPPORT HOURS.] I'm in the Eastern time zone."
**"Why should I trust a solo founder?"** "You shouldn't, yet. That's why there's a free trial, a demo mode that needs no account, and [reference calls]. I'm a working worship pastor running Sunday at a real church, and I read every email."

## Questions to ask them (discovery)

1. What do you run on your TVs today, and who sets it up on Sunday?
2. How many rooms, lobby screens and campuses?
3. What breaks first on a Sunday?
4. Who decides and who pays? (Kids pastor, tech director, admin)
5. What does one hour of Sunday morning troubleshooting cost you in volunteers?

## Close (soft)

"Want to try one room this week? I'll stay on text or email while you pair it."

## When it's not a fit (be generous)

* They need Apple TV, or a built-in curriculum library, or a Planning Center link today.
* They need a main-stage production rig.
* One screen, no time, no appetite: suggest the free demo mode and part friends.
