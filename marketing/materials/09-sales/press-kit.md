# Church TV OS: press kit

**Church TV OS runs the screens at your church (kids rooms, lobby signs and Sunday slides) on the TVs you already have, from your phone.**

Live on Fire TV, Google TV and Roku. LG and Samsung coming soon.

**Press contact:** Keith Sparrow · support@churchtvos.com · [PHONE, optional]
**Website:** churchtvos.com · **Dashboard:** app.churchtvos.com

## Short description (25 words)
Church TV OS runs church kids rooms, lobby signs and Sunday slides on Fire TV, Google TV and Roku, scheduled from the web and run from your phone.

## Medium description (80 words)
Church TV OS puts a church's kids-room curriculum, lobby announcements and Sunday slides on the TVs it already owns. A TV pairs with a six-character code; everything else is built in a web dashboard that works on a phone. One TV can switch between Kids, Signage and Slides on a schedule. TVs download content ahead of time and keep playing if the internet drops, and volunteers can run a room without being able to edit it. Live on Fire TV, Google TV and Roku. LG and Samsung are coming soon.

## Boilerplate
Church TV OS is a screen system for churches and nonprofits, covering kids ministry rooms, digital signage and presentation slides. Learn more at churchtvos.com.

## Founder
**Keith Sparrow** is a worship pastor in Cambridge, Ontario, where he leads the music and tech teams at Forward Church. He built Church TV OS to run the church's kids rooms and lobby screens, and it is now open to any church. Church TV OS is a separate product owned by Keith; Forward Church is a customer.
