# Case study template: Forward Church (customer #1) and any later church

**Rules**
* Real words, real numbers, dated. Nothing here is to be invented. Every blank in brackets must be filled from a real source or deleted.
* **Written permission** before publishing any name, quote or photo: a short email saying "yes, you may quote this with my name and church", dated and saved.
* **Forward Church:** Keith owns Church TV OS personally and Forward Church is customer #1. Get the **written note from Forward Church leadership** (HANDOFF open item) before publishing a case study that names the church. Don't use Forward Church's logo or branding as an endorsement without written agreement, and don't present the church as endorsing the product. Disclose that the founder works at the church: "Full disclosure: Keith Sparrow, who built Church TV OS, is the worship pastor at Forward Church."
* No identifiable children in photos or video. Screens only, hands, backs of TVs.
* Date every metric: "as of [DATE]".

---

## Interview questions (30 minutes; record with permission)

1. Tell me about a Sunday morning before. What did setup look like? Who did it?
2. How many rooms, screens, volunteers and campuses?
3. What was the thing that finally made you change?
4. What did you try first? What happened?
5. What does a Sunday look like now?
6. What's the volunteer's experience?
7. Did anything go wrong? How was it handled?
8. If you hadn't switched, what would you be doing now?
9. What would you tell a church like yours thinking about it?
10. Is there anything you wish it did that it doesn't?
