## Where Church TV OS is a good fit
* You've got Fire sticks, Roku or Google TV boxes (or want to buy cheap ones).
* You want one tool across the kids rooms, lobby and a chapel or overflow room.
* You want volunteers to run rooms from a phone without breaking them.
* You want to see the status of every screen in one place.

## Where something else is a better fit
* You need Apple TV specifically.
* You want a built-in curriculum library or a link to a specific curriculum company.
* You run a large production stage with a dedicated operator and need that depth.
* You only need free, single-room kids playback and you're happy with what you have.
* You need Planning Center or CCLI integration today (there isn't one).

## Honest limits
Newer Fire TV sticks on Vega OS aren't supported · Roku is new · LG and Samsung are coming soon · no curriculum or Planning Center integration · iPhone and Mac apps are coming soon.

## How to word comparisons if you ever publish them
* Say "checked on [DATE]" and link the source. Offer to correct errors: "If we've got something wrong, email support@churchtvos.com and we'll fix it."
* Compare facts, not feelings. No "better than", no screenshots or logos of others, no implied endorsement.
* Prefer category language ("Apple TV based tools") in headlines.
