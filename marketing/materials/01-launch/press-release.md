## Church TV OS launches on Fire TV, Google TV and Roku, bringing kids-room, lobby and sanctuary screens under one phone-friendly system

*A worship pastor in Cambridge, Ontario built it to stop running Sunday on laptops and USB sticks. LG and Samsung versions are coming.*

**CAMBRIDGE, Ontario.** Church TV OS, a screen-management system for churches, is now available on Amazon Fire TV, Google TV and Roku. It lets a church run its kids rooms, lobby signage and Sunday slides on the TVs and streaming devices it already owns, scheduled from the web and controlled from a phone.

Church TV OS has three modules. **Kids** plays curriculum playlists, countdowns, song lyrics and video in each room, on a weekly schedule. **Signage** loops announcements, pictures and video on lobby and hallway screens, in landscape or portrait. **Slides** runs service decks with songs, lyrics, backgrounds and countdowns, with PDF and PowerPoint import. One TV can switch between modules on a schedule, for example signage during the week and kids content on Sunday morning.

A TV pairs with a six-character code. Everything else is built in a web dashboard that works on a phone. TVs download scheduled content ahead of time and keep playing if the internet drops. Volunteers can be given an operator role that lets them step through content and blank the screen but not change anything, with access limited by module and campus.

"Most churches I talk to already have a Fire stick or a Roku in a drawer, and they've been told the good tools need a different box," said Keith Sparrow, creator of Church TV OS and Worship Pastor at Forward Church in Cambridge. [DRAFT QUOTE, APPROVE OR REWRITE] "I wanted the TV a church already owns to do its job all week, and I wanted a volunteer to be able to run it on Sunday without being afraid of it."

Church TV OS began as a system for the kids rooms at Forward Church, where Sparrow leads the music and tech teams. It is a separate product owned by Sparrow, and Forward Church is one of its customers. [CONFIRM wording against the written note from Forward Church leadership before publishing.]

**Availability.** The TV app is free to install from the Amazon Appstore, Google Play and the Roku Channel Store, with no ads. New churches start with a free trial at app.churchtvos.com. Newer Fire TV devices running Amazon's Vega OS are not supported.

**Content and licensing.** Church TV OS plays what a church uploads. Churches remain responsible for licences for lyrics, music, video and curriculum.
