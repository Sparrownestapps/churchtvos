## Body

Hi [FIRST NAME],

It's 8:52 on a Sunday. Somebody is on their knees behind a TV in the nursery looking for the right adapter, and the countdown video is on a laptop that is still in the car.

That Sunday is why I built this.

Church TV OS is live today on **Fire TV, Google TV and Roku**. It runs your kids rooms, your lobby screens and your Sunday slides on TVs you already have, and you run it from your phone.

Here's how it goes:

1. Install Church TV OS on the TV. It shows a six-character code.
2. Type the code into the dashboard, pick the room, done. The TV is yours.
3. Build and schedule what you want. Thursday night is fine.
4. On Sunday, press Next. That's the job.

A few things I care about, because I run Sunday too:

* **It keeps playing if the Wi-Fi drops.** The TV downloads what's scheduled ahead of time.
* **Volunteers can run it and can't break it.** The operator role gets Next, Back, Blank and messages. It can't edit anything.
* **One TV, a whole week.** Signage in the lobby Monday to Saturday. Kids on Sunday morning. Same screen.
* **Bring what you already have.** Import a PDF or PowerPoint, add your own videos, keep your own song lyrics.

A few honest notes so nothing surprises you:

* LG and Samsung are coming. Not live yet. If that's your TV, [join the short list here](LINK: [WAITLIST LINK]) and I'll email you once.
* Newer Fire TV sticks running Amazon's Vega OS can't run it. Google TV boxes and older and current Fire TV models that run standard Fire OS can.
* The Roku channel is brand new. If you try it, tell me what you hit. I read every reply.
