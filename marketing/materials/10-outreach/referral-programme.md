# Referral programme copy

**Status:** copy only. The programme doesn't exist until pricing and a free-month mechanism exist. All rewards are placeholders. From strategy 03 section 6: **"Give a month, get a month."** No cash. No per-sign-up rewards (they tempt fake accounts). Reward only after the referred church **pairs a TV and stays 30 days**.

## Programme basics (decide before launch)

| Item | Placeholder |
|---|---|
| Referrer reward | `[REWARD: one free month per referred church that pairs a TV and stays 30 days]` |
| New church benefit | `[EXTRA TRIAL DAYS: 30 extra days]` |
| Cap | `[MAX REWARD per year]` |
| Who counts | Referred church must be a new, separate church, not the same organisation |
| Tracking | A unique referral link per church: `https://churchtvos.com/?ref=[CODE]&utm_source=referral&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=launch2027`. No personal data in the URL. |
| Fraud rule | One reward per referred church; no self-referral; no multiple accounts |

## Landing-page / dashboard block

**Headline:** Give a month, get a month.
**Text:** Know a church whose Sunday could use a calmer screen setup? Share your link. When they pair a TV and stay 30 days, you both get something: you get [REWARD], they get [EXTRA TRIAL DAYS].
**Button:** Copy my link
**Fine print:** One reward per new church. Rewards are applied to your next invoice and aren't paid out in cash. See the [terms].

## Business referral (consultants, integrators, networks)

* **Agreement in writing** before they refer anyone: terms, reward, cap, duration, how it's paid (credit, not cash, unless you decide otherwise), the disclosure wording, and how either side can end it.
* **Disclosure wording they must use:** "I receive a referral benefit if you try Church TV OS." Placed where the church sees it.
* **No exclusive claims, no pressure tactics, no fear-based copy.** Strategy 03 section 10.
* Offer: `[BUSINESS REFERRAL TERMS: placeholder]`.

## Founding-church link to referral
Founding churches can be first referrers. Keep it separate: the founding offer is `[FOUNDING OFFER]`; the referral reward is `[REWARD]`. Stack them only if you have decided they stack.

## Not allowed
* Paying for or incentivising store reviews (the review request email stays separate, no reward).
* Rewarding clicks, sign-ups, or social posts.
* Asking referrers to post on behalf of the church without disclosure.
* Using Forward Church's name in referral copy.

## Measure
Referrals sent, referred churches that pair a TV, rewards earned, fraud rejects. Target from strategy 03: 0.2 referred churches per paying church in year one (hypothesis).
