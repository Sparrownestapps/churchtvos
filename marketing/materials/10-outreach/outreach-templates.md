# Outreach templates

Every message here is **one-to-one, relevant, identifies you, and offers an unsubscribe or an easy "no thanks".** That keeps it on the right side of CASL (and CAN-SPAM for US recipients): it is still a commercial message if it promotes the product, so include the identity line. **Never** mass-send, buy a list, or email a stranger "to see what happens". Nothing here has been sent.

**Before sending any:** read the recipient's own submission or contact page (some podcasts and newsletters publish pitch rules). Personalise the first line with something real about them. Delete anything that isn't true for that person.
**Identity line (put at the bottom of every email):** Keith Sparrow · Church TV OS · churchtvos.com · [POSTAL ADDRESS] · "If you'd rather not hear from me, just reply 'no thanks' and I won't write again."
**Tracking:** keep a simple sheet: name, outlet, date sent, date of reply, outcome. Follow up once, five to seven days later. Then stop.

**Verified targets from the research (check each is still active; do not assume contact details):**
Church tech: ChurchTechToday (newsletter and podcast; the site lists a contact address), Church Tech Weekly, Church IT Network, Church Production Magazine / podcast (research says it may be inactive), ChurchLeaders ministry-tech section. YouTube: Churchfront, Pro Church Tools. Kids ministry podcasts: Simple Kidmin, Kids Ministry Circle, KidzMatter, Orange Kids Podcast, Kidmin Talk, Kids Ministry 101. Canada: regional PAOC, Baptist, Alliance, CRC and EFC kids-ministry networks. (Sources and confidence labels are in `marketing/01-market-research.md` section 4.)
