**Use for:** a /coming-soon page, a form on the launch landing page, social replies to "when is it on my TV?", and the in-store "coming soon" mentions. No dates are promised. We do not control LG or Samsung review timelines.
**Rule:** never write "coming in [month]". Write "coming soon" and "we'll email you once, when it's ready".
