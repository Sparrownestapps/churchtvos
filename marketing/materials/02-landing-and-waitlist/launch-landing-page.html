How it works

From box to Sunday in three steps

  1. Pair a TV

    Open the app on the TV. It shows a six-character code. Type it into the dashboard, pick the room, and the TV is yours.

    NCB Z3C

  2. Build and schedule

    Upload videos and pictures, write songs and signs, or import a PDF or PowerPoint. Put it on the calendar. Thursday night is fine.

  3. Run it from your phone

    Next, back, jump to any slide, blank, or send a message to the screen. Volunteers get an operator role that can run the room but can't edit anything.

Questions, answered plainly

What TV hardware do I need?

A Fire TV, Google TV or Android TV, or Roku device plugged into the TV, running the Church TV OS app. Newer Fire TV sticks that run Amazon's Vega OS can't run it. LG and Samsung apps are coming soon.

What if the internet goes down on Sunday?

The TV downloads what's scheduled ahead of time, so it keeps playing from saved content and catches up when the connection returns.

Can volunteers run it without breaking it?

Yes. The operator role can use the remote (next, back, blank, play now, messages) but can't edit playlists, decks, schedules or screens.

Can I bring in my PowerPoint or PDF?

Yes. PDF pages become full-screen picture slides that keep your layout. PowerPoint slides become editable text slides with your notes. Animations aren't carried over, so for the exact look, export to PDF first.

Does Roku work the same as Fire TV?

Mostly, but the Roku channel is new. Some things differ: for example, YouTube items aren't supported on Roku. If you try it, tell me what you find.

Whose music, lyrics and videos does it play?

Yours. Church TV OS plays what you upload. Your church is responsible for the licences for the lyrics, music, video and curriculum you add.

Is our content kept separate from other churches?

Yes. Everything belongs to your church and nothing is shared between churches. Read the privacy policy.
