# 30-day social calendar

Day 1 is launch day (a Tuesday). Shift every date with it. Nothing here has been posted or scheduled.

**How to use this**
* 15 minutes a day is the plan. Most days have one "main" post and one or two small ones. If you only have time for one channel, do the Facebook page and the group comments.
* **Facebook groups: ask the admins first** (one short DM, text at the bottom). Never post a link in a group that bans links. Never post the same text in two groups. If a group bans self-promotion, use the "comment seed" only, answer questions, and let your profile do the talking. Disclose in the first line every time.
* **Children:** no photos or video of identifiable children, ever. Photograph screens, hands on a remote, the back of a TV.
* **Real proof only.** Posts marked `[PROOF]` need a real Forward Church or beta-church photo, number or quote with permission. If you don't have it yet, swap in the "Screens" alternative shown.
* **Every link** gets a UTM tag from the scheme in strategy 03 section 7. Format: `https://churchtvos.com/?utm_source=SOURCE&utm_medium=MEDIUM&utm_campaign=launch2027&utm_content=ASSET`
* Images are in `../08-graphics/out/` (names given). Short video ideas reference the cuts in `../04-video/`.
* Hashtag sets and caption variants are in `hashtags-and-caption-variants.md`.
* Reply to every comment the same day in the first two weeks. That is the real marketing.

**Pillars:** S = Sunday setup (how-to) · R = Real rooms (proof) · T = Practical church tech (teaching) · B = Behind the build (founder) · O = Offers and updates (low volume)
