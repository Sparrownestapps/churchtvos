## Caption variants: the three core posts

### 1. Launch post

**Short (X, Instagram story, 140 characters or fewer):**
> Church TV OS is live on Fire TV, Google TV and Roku. Run your church screens from your phone. churchtvos.com

**Medium (Facebook page, Instagram):**
> It's live. Church TV OS runs your kids rooms, lobby signs and Sunday slides on the TVs you already own. Pair with a six-character code. Run the room from your phone.
> Live on Fire TV, Google TV and Roku. LG and Samsung coming soon. Free trial.

**Long (LinkedIn, blog teaser):**
> Picture ten to nine on a Sunday morning. Somebody is behind the nursery TV hunting for an adapter, and the countdown video is on a laptop that's still in the car. I built Church TV OS so that scene is rare. It runs kids rooms, lobby signage and Sunday slides on the TVs you already have. You set it up on the web, pair a TV with a six-character code, and run the room from your phone. It's live today on Fire TV, Google TV and Roku. LG and Samsung are coming. [LINK]

**Story-led (Facebook groups, only where allowed):**
> Disclosure: I made this. We were running our kids rooms off laptops and USB sticks and I got tired of Sunday morning cable hunts. So I built a way to run them from a Fire stick on each TV, scheduled from the web. Happy to share the checklist, no strings.

### 2. "Set it Thursday, forget it Sunday"

**Short:** Set it Thursday. Forget it Sunday. Schedule each room ahead of time and the TV does the rest.
**Medium:** Schedule each kids room on Thursday. The TV downloads it ahead of time, keeps playing if the Wi-Fi drops, and shows the countdown on Sunday without anyone carrying a laptop.
**Long:** The best Sunday morning is the one where nothing happens. That takes a little work on Thursday: schedule each room, check the screen status from your phone, give your volunteers the operator role. Then on Sunday there's nothing to fix, only Next to press.

### 3. "Your volunteers can't break it"

**Short:** Give a volunteer one job: press Next. The operator role can't edit anything.
**Medium:** A volunteer who's afraid of the nursery TV is a volunteer you might lose. The operator role gets Next, Back, Blank, Play now and messages, and nothing else. They can run the room. They can't break it.
**Long:** I've watched good people freeze in front of a remote with forty kids watching. So on Church TV OS the operator role is deliberately small: step through, blank the screen, send a short message. No editing, no scheduling, no way to wipe Sunday by accident. Editors and admins do the building. Operators do the running.
