## 4. Review and rating tactics (ethical only)
* Google TV: add the in-app review prompt after meaningful use (for example three completed Sundays), idle UI, at most once per period.
* Fire TV and Roku: a QR code in Settings linking to the store page (a build change, not marketing copy).
* Email beta and trial churches **after** they've used it on a Sunday: "If it helped, a short honest review helps other churches find it. If not, tell me." No incentives. VERIFY each store's current review-solicitation policy before sending.
* Reply to every review within 48 hours, good or bad.
* Target 15 honest reviews in 90 days.

## 5. A/B copy tests
Change only the first sentence of the description every 4 weeks: (A) "Run your kids rooms, lobby signs and Sunday slides…" (B) "Your kids rooms, lobby and Sunday slides, on the TVs you already own…" (C) "A six-character code is the whole setup…". Record installs and trial starts per variant.
